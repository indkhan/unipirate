Uniweg design system v1.0 · verified 03 Jul 2026

Wayfinding

A visual system for guiding students from India, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia to German public universities. Borrowed from German public wayfinding and document culture: transit-map clarity, signage typography, stamps that mean something. Official but warm. Precise but calm. The page is paper, the words are ink, the route is blue, truth is green, time is amber.

01

Palette

#F2F4F7
Paper
App background. Cool paper-neutral — never cream, never pure white. Cards sit on it in white Surface.
#16243D
Ink
All text. A navy-black — never #000. Secondary and muted steps for supporting copy and timestamps.
#1F4FBF
Route Blue
The one brand color: primary actions, links, the Route line, "you are here", focus, selection. If it isn't interactive or part of the journey, it isn't blue.
#0E7A4B
Verified Green
Reserved. Only for verified, source-checked states — the Verified stamp. Never buttons, never links, never generic success.
#E5A32E / #9A6200
Signal Amber
Reserved. Only for time pressure — deadlines, expiring documents, stale verification. Text uses the dark step; marks use the bright one.
#B3261E
Danger
Form errors and destructive actions only. A missed deadline is red; an approaching one is amber.
02

Type — three voices

The sign
Archivo

Display. Headlines, station names, card titles, buttons. Chosen over Barlow: sturdier DIN-flavored forms — flat terminals, tall x-height — that hold up at signage sizes without reading startup-generic.

The clerk
Public Sans

Body. Explanations, help text, forms. Built for the U.S. federal government: official but plain, and it stays legible at 14–16px on cheap Android panels.

The document
IBM Plex Mono

Data. Dates, deadlines, rule IDs, citations, file numbers. If a stamp would touch it, it is mono. This voice is what makes "verified, with sources and dates" visible.

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. Uppercase belongs to mono labels only. Body never below 14px. Dates are always DD MMM YYYY, always mono. No emoji, no exclamation marks.

03

The Route — signature element

The student's journey as a transit line. Done stations are filled blue with a check, the current station carries the "you are here" marker (the system's only looping motion — it stops under reduced motion), upcoming stations are hollow. Horizontal from 768px up, vertical on mobile. One per screen; everything around it stays quiet.

Vertical · mobile default
✓ Done — filled Route Blue with a check. Progress is blue, not green.
You are here — ringed marker with a soft halo pulse, 2.4s.
Upcoming — hollow dot, dashed segment, muted label. Not scary, just not yet.
04

The Verified stamp — secondary motif

The only home of Verified Green. A quality seal: check, source link, last-verified date — all in the document voice. It appears wherever a rule is cited, and nowhere it isn't earned. No source and date, no stamp.

Chip · inline, everyday

Sits under claims, inside checklist rows, next to deadlines. Source is a dotted-underline link.

Stamp · hero moments, max one per screen

The −1.5° rotation is the system's one moment of hand-applied warmth.

05

Components

Buttons
Check my eligibility See how it works Skip for now

Archivo 600, sentence case, 48px tap target. One primary per screen region. Hover darkens; press darkens one more step — nothing fades or shrinks. Green is never a button.

Form inputs

48px tall, 16px value text (no Android focus-zoom). Required is written out, never a bare asterisk. Errors say what to do.

Task checklist

Tap anywhere on the row. Checked is Route Blue with a strikethrough — progress is blue; green stays reserved. Deadline chips and Verified stamps slot in below the detail line.

Deadline chips

The only place Signal Amber appears. Within 14 days the countdown shows; missed flips the whole chip to Danger. Amber means "time pressure", red means "missed".

Progress states

For countable sets. Journey-level progress belongs to the Route, not a bar.

Empty state

Dashed border, hollow station dot — "no stops recorded yet". Calm copy, one action, no sad illustrations.

Documents have corners: 6px controls, 10px cards, pills only for station dots. Elevation is flat — hairlines do the separating. One shadow token exists, for dialogs and menus only.

Motion policy
140ms — state changes: checks, focus, chips. 240ms — reveals: sheets, accordions, progress fills. One signature move: the Route line drawing toward "you are here" on dashboard load. Nothing loops except the here-marker pulse; nothing bounces; nothing is decorative. All of it sits behind prefers-reduced-motion.
