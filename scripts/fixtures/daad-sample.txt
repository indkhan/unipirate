Description/content
The programme imparts knowledge of modern data analysis and machine learning methods, combining theory with practical application. Focus areas include data analytics, operations research, weather and climate research, and mathematical foundations, enabling students to solve complex data problems in scientific and industrial contexts.

Course organisation

The Master's programme comprises four semesters of full-time study.

Academic admission requirements
A Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, or Physics (or closely related field) formally equivalent to a German academic Bachelor's is required. GRE Subject Test in Mathematics is mandatory for applicants from Bangladesh, China, India, Iran, and Pakistan.
